2. Tongue face set 0.45 behind the PCB edge with a 0.60 bump (0.15 preload), so the rigid tongue root never touches the PCB at full float (0.15 clear). The contract says "0.15 bump"; read here as the 0.15 preload. The tongue bottom is at 119.05, not 118.65, so the front tongue keeps 0.44 from the seated carrier flange (>= 0.40).
